# Authorship, provenance and scope

This pack is an independent educational extension inspired by **Project: NAYA**, James Bourke's university capstone at Deakin University. The original project explored TVC on a bench demonstrator. Its mention explains the motivation; this pack does not distribute its final engineering solution.

All example parameters and controller gains were chosen or calculated afresh for this public teaching model. No NAYA gains, physical dimensions, detailed model, calibration, raw test logs, identification results, hardware code, CAD, wiring, mechanisms or operating procedures are included. The numerical outputs describe this generic simulation only.

The attached assessment instructions and status templates were inspected for context but are not included or licensed for redistribution. No university logo, employer material, collaborator-owned work or third-party photograph is bundled. A contextual NAYA photograph has not been added in this first pass.
